    How a verdict is made

    StatusMeaning
    PassEvery input was available and every ratio is within its limit.
    FailA business screen failed, or a financial ratio is over its limit.
    Needs reviewA borderline business case. It is never passed automatically. A reviewer may resolve it with a recorded override.
    Insufficient dataA required input is missing or out of date. It is never treated as a pass.

    The checks run in this order, and the first that applies decides: a business fail, then missing or out-of-date input, then a ratio over its limit, then a borderline business case. Otherwise the result is a pass. A few checks can turn a pass into insufficient data; none changes a fail or a review.

    The method in brief

    TestLimitRule
    Interest-bearing debt / market capbelow 30%Strict "<". Debt from the widest set of tags that does not double count.
    Cash and interest-bearing securities / market capbelow 30%Strict "<". Same rule as debt.
    Impure income / revenuebelow 5%Strict "<". Interest income only.
    Business screenfailAlcohol, tobacco, gambling, conventional banks and insurers, and weapons makers. Some borderline groups go to needs review.
    Market cap12-month averageVerdicts use the 12-month average of daily market cap. Spot and the 36-month average are stored and compared.
    Near a limitwithin 10%A passing ratio within 10% of its limit, measured relative to the limit, is flagged.

    Filings are read only if they were filed on or before the screen date, and the screen date's own price is never read. Stored history only moves forward. The full method, and the points still to verify, are in the README.

    Limits

    • Most companies do not tag interest income. Other bases are labelled lower confidence on the page.
    • The business screen cannot see revenue mix inside a harmless industry code.
    • There is no historical backtest. The constituent list and industry codes are today's values.
    • The thresholds and the business rules are the project's own reading. They are not yet checked against the AAOIFI text.

    The full list is in the README.
