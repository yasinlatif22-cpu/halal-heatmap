    How a screen is applied

    ResultMeaning
    Meets all criteriaEvery metric in the profile was available and met its threshold.
    Fails a criterionAt least one metric is available and does not meet its threshold.
    Metric unavailableNo criterion failed, but a metric could not be computed. It is never treated as a pass.

    Criteria are evaluated in your browser on the exported metrics, so editing a threshold changes the map at once and nothing is sent anywhere. A reference ratio screen with industry exclusions, defined in config.yaml, is also available as a separate view.

    The method in brief

    Metric groupExamplesRule
    ProfitabilityGross, operating and net marginTrailing twelve months of the relevant income line / trailing revenue.
    ReturnsReturn on equity, return on assetsTrailing net income / equity or assets at the latest balance sheet. Undefined for non-positive equity.
    Leverage and liquidityDebt / equity, interest coverage, cash / market capDebt from the widest set of tags that does not double count. Coverage is undefined without positive interest expense.
    Cash flow and valuationFree cash flow yield, earnings yield, P/E, P/S, P/BFree cash flow is operating cash flow less capital expenditures. Multiples are undefined for non-positive denominators.
    Market cap12-month average by defaultClosing price times reported shares. Spot and the 36-month average are stored and compared.

    Filings are read only if they were filed on or before the screen date, and the screen date's own price is never read. Stored history only moves forward. A metric is shown as unavailable when its input is not reported; nothing is estimated. The full method is in the README.

    Limits

    • Companies tag the same item differently. The tag lists in config.yaml cover the common cases; where none matches, the metric is unavailable.
    • Metrics are point-in-time ratios from the latest filings. There is no historical backtest here, and the constituent list and industry codes are today's values.
    • Financial companies report differently. Margins, leverage and coverage are not comparable between a bank and an industrial company.
    • The example profiles are illustrative thresholds, not recommendations or a view on any company.

    The full list is in the README.

    Equity Screener is an automated research tool. It is not investment advice, and it does not recommend any security. Read the filing behind any figure before relying on it.
